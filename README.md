# Amos Matzke

Founder of AM Creative Tech UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Dresden · Building Quanta Study and reusable TypeScript components.

I develop [Quanta Study](https://quanta-study.de/en), a web-based, AI-assisted learning platform that connects personal study materials with active exam preparation. Learners can create flashcards manually or turn their documents into cards, quizzes, summaries and learning podcasts. Available source references help them check generated material; free-answer practice with an AI tutor provides follow-up questions and feedback. FSRS flashcard reviews and study planning bring that work into a regular learning routine.

My work spans TypeScript, React, Next.js, Firebase and AI processing, including source references, streaming interfaces and technical SEO/AEO implementation.

## Quanta Study

- [Reuse existing Anki and CSV text cards](https://quanta-study.de/en/import-flashcards) as new cards with a fresh Quanta review schedule.
- [Create learning podcasts from study materials](https://quanta-study.de/en/pdf-to-podcast), with a manuscript that can be reviewed and edited before audio generation.
- [Explore the public formula reference](https://quanta-study.de/en/formula-sheet), with explanations, calculators and STEM tools.

Quanta Study is developed and operated by AM Creative Tech UG (haftungsbeschränkt). Its public learning resources and institutional options complement the personal learning workspace.
